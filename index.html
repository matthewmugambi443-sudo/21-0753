The High-End Fashion Checklist

  1. The Heritage Maisons (The Big Three)

  2. Italian Craftsmanship & Glamour
    • Gucci (Maximalist, bold, and culturally dominant)
    • Prada (Intellectual luxury and nylon innovation)

  3. "Quiet Luxury" & Stealth Wealth

    • Maison Margiela (Deconstructionist and experimental)
    • Rick Owens (Dark, architectural, and "glunge")
    • Balenciaga (Boundary-pushing shapes and high-concept streetwear)
  5. Modern Luxury & Redefined Classics
    • Saint Laurent (Rock-and-roll edge and sharp tailoring)
    • Loewe (Spanish heritage met with quirky, artistic design)
    • Jacquemus (Playful, sun-drenched Mediterranean luxury)
